Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Who we are

AdeStudioPrintables, based in France. Contact: adestudio.printables@gmail.com.

What this website collects

This website has no account, no contact form, no advertising and no tracking or analytics cookies. It does not collect your personal data itself.

Two technical services see your IP address when the page loads: our host, GitHub Pages, which keeps short server logs for security, and Google Fonts, which provides the text fonts.

Free gift and purchases

When you get the free winter pages or buy a product, you leave this website and use Gumroad, Etsy or Payhip. They collect the details needed for your order, such as your name, email address and payment details, under their own privacy policies.

For the free winter pages, Gumroad shares your email address with us. We use it only to send your download and occasional news about new printables and offers from AdeStudioPrintables. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link in each email or by writing to us. We never sell or share your email address.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, and for no more than three years after your last contact with us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Write to adestudio.printables@gmail.com and we will answer within one month. You can also complain to the CNIL, the French data protection authority, at cnil.fr.

Politique de confidentialité (version française)

Ce site ne collecte pas de données personnelles et n'utilise aucun cookie de suivi ni de publicité. L'hébergeur (GitHub Pages) et Google Fonts voient votre adresse IP lors du chargement de la page.

Pour les pages gratuites et les achats, vous passez par Gumroad, Etsy ou Payhip, qui collectent les données nécessaires selon leurs propres politiques. Pour les pages gratuites, Gumroad nous transmet votre adresse e-mail : nous l'utilisons uniquement pour vous envoyer le téléchargement et, de temps en temps, nos nouveautés. Désinscription possible à tout moment. Nous ne vendons ni ne partageons jamais votre adresse.

Conservation : jusqu'à votre désinscription, et au maximum trois ans après votre dernier contact. Vous pouvez demander l'accès, la rectification ou la suppression de vos données à adestudio.printables@gmail.com, ou saisir la CNIL (cnil.fr).
